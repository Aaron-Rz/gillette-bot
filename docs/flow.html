Sam – How the bot works

Sam leads a Delhaize shopper in six steps from the first hello to a confirmed order and a reason to come back. The three diagrams show the journey, the decision tree behind every turn, and how the bot talks to the CRM and the other systems.

1Conversion journey

The six steps from left to right, one lane per actor or system. Logged-in SuperPlus members skip most questions because the CRM already knows them; guests build the same profile from what they type, a photo or the beard try-on.

2Decision tree

Diamonds are decisions, boxes are actions. The left side is the guided path from entry to follow-up; the panel on the right is the router that every typed message passes through, in any step, before Sam acts, asks “Did you mean…?” or shows the topic menu.

Swipe sideways inside the diagram.

3System map

One dialog core serves every channel. It asks the understanding layer what a message means, reads and writes the SuperPlus CRM, and calls the catalogue, store list and order systems. Camera images stay on the device; only derived findings reach the dialog, and reminders pass a consent gate.
